Good to know

Questions, answered.

How is my shipping cost calculated?

Air freight is charged by the greater of actual weight or volumetric weight (length × width × height in cm ÷ 6000). Sea freight is charged by weight or by cubic metre (CBM) for groupage. Use the calculator above for an instant estimate.

Who pays Nigerian customs duty?

Import duty, VAT and any regulated-goods permits (NAFDAC, SONCAP) are separate from freight and are the consignee's responsibility. For commercial cargo we help arrange Form M, PAAR and clearance through our licensed clearing partners in Nigeria.

What can't I ship?

Nigeria's import prohibition list bans certain goods, and used vehicles over 12 years old are not permitted. Hazardous materials, cash and counterfeit goods are never accepted. Ask us before booking if you're unsure.

Do you collect from my home?

Yes — we offer nationwide UK collection, or you can drop off at our hub. In Nigeria we offer warehouse collection or door-to-door delivery to most major cities.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer or card once your quote is confirmed and goods are weighed. You'll receive a tracking number and updates throughout the journey.
